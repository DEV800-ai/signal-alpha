# NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA)

## 1. Strategic position

NVDA designs the GPUs and networking silicon that train the largest AI models.
Today it is the dominant supplier to every western hyperscaler, with Blackwell
(B200 / GB200) shipping at scale and the Rubin generation entering customer
sampling. Software (CUDA, NeMo, DGX Cloud) is the moat that makes hardware
switching costs real. Custom-ASIC competitors (TPU, MTIA, MAIA) take share at
the margins but do not yet displace NVDA's position in frontier training.

## 2. Bull case

- Hyperscaler 2026 capex guidance points to >$300B aggregate, with NVDA
  capturing the largest share of compute spend
  ([Q1 FY27 commentary](transcript:NVDA:2026-Q1#capex)).
- Blackwell-Ultra (B300) ramp begins H2 2026; cycle compression vs. prior gen
  suggests revenue acceleration
  ([Q4 FY26 release](earnings:NVDA:2026-02-25)).
- Sovereign AI deals (~$50B booked across UAE / KSA / EU) are multi-year,
  locking in capacity ([Q3 FY26 call](transcript:NVDA:2025-11-19#sovereign)).

## 3. Bear case

- Hyperscaler custom-ASIC roadmaps (Trainium 3, MAIA 2, TPUv7) reach inference
  parity in 2026, compressing the inference TAM.
- China export controls extended to "performance-equivalent" silicon — fore-
  closes the H20 workaround channel
  ([Reuters](news:reuters/2026-01-15/nvidia-h20-controls)).
- Concentration risk: top 4 customers > 40% of data-center revenue
  ([10-Q FY26 Q4](filing:0001045810-26-000012#concentration)).

## 4. What is priced in

<!-- claim_type: interpretation -->

The consensus prices in flawless Blackwell ramp, sustained data-center gross
margins above the mid-70s, and zero share loss to custom ASICs through 2027.
Any execution miss on Rubin sampling or any hyperscaler "diversification"
headline gets punished asymmetrically — the upside-surprise bar is now very
high relative to history.
